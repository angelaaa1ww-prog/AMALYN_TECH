About AMALYN TECH
AMALYN TECH is an AI-driven audio intelligence system designed to eliminate human error and technical failure in live sound reinforcement and studio production. It acts as a Digital Co-Pilot — analyzing audio signals in real time, predicting problems before they happen, and automatically pushing corrections to the mixer.
Core Capabilities
🧠
AI Feedback Detection
AMALYN uses Fast Fourier Transform to analyze every frequency in real time. Two detection layers — a threshold system and a trained neural network — work simultaneously to catch feedback before it reaches damaging levels.
📚
Brand Frequency Library
A built-in database of known frequency response curves for industry-leading speaker, microphone and mixer brands. AMALYN uses this to give more precise corrections tailored to your exact equipment.
⭐
Perfect State System
Before the show starts, tell AMALYN your venue type, speaker model, microphone and mixer. It calculates the ideal EQ settings for that exact combination and loads them as a starting point.
🛡️
AMALYN Sentinel
A hardware health monitor that watches signal stability, clipping rates, dropout patterns and noise floor. If a cable is about to fail, Sentinel predicts it and alerts you before the audio cuts out.
🎛️
OSC Mixer Integration
When AMALYN detects a problem, it doesn't just warn you. It sends the EQ correction directly to the mixer via OSC protocol — cutting the problem frequency automatically in real time.
🤖
Machine Learning
AMALYN trains a neural network on your own real audio data. The more you use it, the smarter it gets — learning the acoustic patterns of your specific environments and equipment.
How It Works
1
Signal Capture
AMALYN opens an audio stream from your microphone or audio interface at 44.1kHz, 16-bit. Every 512 samples (~11ms) it captures a new frame of audio.
2
FFT Analysis
Fast Fourier Transform converts the time-domain audio into a frequency map — showing the magnitude of every frequency from 20Hz to 20kHz in real time.
3
Dual Detection
Two systems run simultaneously — a threshold detector that watches for frequencies exceeding -25dBFS, and a trained neural network that recognizes the pattern of feedback building earlier.
4
EQ Calculation
When danger is detected, AMALYN calculates the exact correction — frequency, cut depth in dB, Q value and filter type — based on the specific equipment in the library.
5
Mixer Push
The correction is sent to the connected mixer via OSC over the local network. The mixer applies the EQ cut automatically — no manual intervention required.

How To Use AMALYN TECH
🚀 Getting Started
1
Start the APIOpen PowerShell, activate your environment and run py -3.11 api.py from the AMALYN_AI folder. Wait for [ENGINE] Audio engine started.
2
Sign InOpen login.html in Chrome or Edge. Sign in with your credentials or use a demo account. Never use Go Live or Live Server — open the file directly.
3
Choose Your PortalSelect the portal that matches your role — Engineer Dashboard for live sound, Musician Portal for IEM control, Producer Portal for studio analysis.
🎛️ Engineer — Live Event Workflow
1
Load Perfect StateClick Setup in the dashboard. Select your venue type, speaker system, microphone and mixer. Click Load Perfect State — AMALYN pre-loads the ideal EQ corrections for your exact setup.
2
Monitor the SpectrumWatch the live spectrum during soundcheck. The display updates 40 times per second. Green means clean. Orange means warning. Red means critical.
3
Let AMALYN WorkWhen feedback is detected, AMALYN shows the exact frequency and recommended cut — and pushes the correction to your mixer automatically via OSC. You can also apply it manually on your desk.
4
Watch SentinelThe Signal Health score shows 0-100. If it drops, Sentinel has detected instability — check the alert for the specific issue (clipping, dropout, cable failure prediction).
Pro tip: Run the Mixer Simulator (py -3.11 simulator.py) in a second terminal to see every OSC message AMALYN sends to the mixer in real time. This is useful for testing before connecting to a real desk.
🎸 Musician — IEM Monitor Workflow
1
Connect to WiFiMake sure your phone is on the same WiFi network as the AMALYN system.
2
Open Musician PortalFrom the login screen select Musician Portal or ask your engineer for the direct link.
3
Adjust Your MixUse the sliders to set your personal IEM mix. Drag right for more, left for less. Tap MUT to mute a channel. Changes go to the mixer instantly.
🤖 ML Training — Make AMALYN Smarter
1
Collect DataRun py -3.11 ml_collector.py. Label audio as CLEAN (Enter), WARNING (w) or CRITICAL (c) while making different sounds. Collect at least 500 frames of each.
2
Train the ModelRun py -3.11 ml_trainer.py. It trains for 50 epochs and saves the best model automatically.
3
It Loads AutomaticallyNext time you start api.py, the ML model loads automatically and runs alongside the threshold detector — catching problems earlier.
Important: The more data you collect in your specific venue with your specific equipment, the more accurate AMALYN becomes. Re-train regularly for best results.
